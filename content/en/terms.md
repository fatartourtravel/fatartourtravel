These terms apply when you request a quotation or book through FATAR Tour & Travel (PT. Fatar Mitra Sarana). They are written for WhatsApp bookings — the site does not process payments.

## Prices on the website

Package prices, currency conversions and car rates on this website are **estimates** to help you plan.

- Package prices are based on MYR and the group size shown
- SGD and IDR displays are conversions and may differ from the final quote
- Hotel availability, ferry times, public holidays and group size can change the total

The price that matters is the quotation we confirm on WhatsApp.

## How a booking is confirmed

A trip is confirmed only after:

1. We send a written quotation on WhatsApp, and  
2. You accept it, and  
3. Any deposit we request has been received

Until then, vehicles, rooms and restaurant slots are not guaranteed.

## What is included

Each quotation will state what is included — for example transport, hotel nights, meals or tickets. Anything not listed is outside the package. Standard and full-package options can differ; ask us to spell out the inclusions for your dates.

## Changes and cancellation

Tell us as early as you can if dates, pax or hotels need to change. We will check what can still be arranged.

Cancellation or date-change fees depend on suppliers (hotels, vehicles, ferries). We will tell you the cost before you confirm a change. Deposits may be non-refundable if a supplier has already been paid.

## Your responsibilities

- Give accurate dates, names and guest counts  
- Share ferry or flight arrival times if you want pickup  
- Keep a copy of the quotation we sent  

## Liability

We arrange local services with care. We are not responsible for delays or changes caused by weather, ferry operators, traffic, or third-party hotels and restaurants. If a supplier cannot deliver, we will try to offer a reasonable alternative.
