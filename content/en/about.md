FATAR Tour & Travel is operated by **PT. Fatar Mitra Sarana**, a Batam-based team that plans private holidays around Batam, Bintan and Penyengat.

FATAR stands for **Family, Adventure, Tourism, Accommodation and Relaxation**. The idea is simple: one local team coordinates the itinerary, hotel, meals and transport, so you spend the trip enjoying the islands — not arranging each piece yourself.

## Who we help

We work with families, couples and private groups, especially travellers arriving from **Singapore**, **Malaysia** and **Indonesia**. Many guests start with a ferry into Batam and want a clear plan before they arrive.

## What we arrange

- Batam and Bintan holiday packages
- Custom itineraries around your dates and group size
- Hotel options that match the trip
- Private cars, minibuses and drivers in Batam
- Restaurant timing so meals sit naturally in the day

## How we work

Send your travel dates and number of guests on WhatsApp. We reply with options and a quotation. Prices on the website are estimates to help you plan; the final figure is confirmed in chat before you pay.

Our tagline is **Perjalanan Lebih Bermakna** — travel that gives your family more time together.
